About

Financial Engineering Laboratory is a research group in the Division of Finance & AI at Hankuk University of Foreign Studies, Global Campus. The lab is led by Prof. Hyeongwoo Kong.

We study how data analytics and machine learning can improve financial decision making. Our work spans financial data science, quantitative modeling of customer behavior and risk, and the evaluation of generative models for financial time series.
